▶ Architecture


285 showcase modules plus 10 preview leads, audited across seven passes. IntersectionObserver gates every card. Named ticker cleanup, no anonymous leaks. ScrollTrigger lives inside GSAP context so unmount kills it.


The marketing film is a separate pipeline: Playwright records the vault being used, FFmpeg cuts intertitles and xfade into a 46s reel. The app never serves those files — library and positioning stay split on purpose.
